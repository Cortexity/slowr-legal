Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Select Slowr and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open Slowr, go through the welcome flow, and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used originally.

What is a reverse Kegel?

A reverse Kegel is the opposite of a regular Kegel — instead of squeezing the pelvic floor, you actively relax and lengthen it on the inhale. Slowr guides you through both movements because real ejaculatory control requires both strength and the ability to relax on command.

How long until I see results?

Most men notice improvements within 21 days of consistent daily training. Significant results typically appear at 4–6 weeks. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Can I use Slowr without the subscription?

Slowr offers a 3-day free trial so you can try the full experience before committing. After the trial, an active subscription is required to continue using the workouts.

Is my data private?

Yes. Slowr does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Your training data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
